Keep a copy of the top of the chain

  • Copy the top hash above into somewhere outside this app now and then (a ticket, an email). If the last events are ever removed or the whole chain is rebuilt, the value you kept will no longer match.
  • Keep the .env file with your database backup. The chain key comes from SECRET_KEY, or from AUDIT_HASH_KEY if you set it.
  • The check alone cannot notice that the very last events were deleted, and someone who holds both the database and the key can rebuild the whole chain. The copy you kept outside the app covers that gap.
